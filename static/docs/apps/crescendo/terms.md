## Description of Service

Crescendo is a practice journal for musicians. It lets you organize the pieces you're working on, record audio and video takes, write timestamped notes, attach YouTube reference videos, and track your performances and setlists. Crescendo is a **local-first application**: your content is stored on your own device, and we do not operate cloud servers that hold your recordings or notes.
