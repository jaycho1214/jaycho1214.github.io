# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** June 8, 2026

## Overview

Crescendo ("we," "our," or "the app") is a practice journal for musicians developed by Jaeyoung Cho. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

## Data Storage

Crescendo is a **local-first application**. Your songs, takes, recordings, notes, performances, setlists, and preferences are stored on your device using SQLite, and your audio and video recordings are saved as files on your device. We do not operate our own cloud servers or databases for storing your content.

## Information We Collect

### Analytics (PostHog)

We use PostHog to collect anonymous usage analytics and error reports. This helps us understand how the app is used and fix bugs. Data collected may include:

- App usage events (e.g., features used, screens viewed)
- Error and crash reports
- Device type, OS version, and app version

This data is sent to PostHog's servers. It does not include your name, email address, or the content of your recordings or notes.

### Purchase Validation (RevenueCat)

We use RevenueCat to validate in-app purchases for Crescendo Pro. When you make a purchase, RevenueCat processes anonymous device and transaction data to verify your purchase with Apple or Google. RevenueCat does not receive your name, email, or payment details.

## Information We Do Not Collect

- We do not collect your name, email address, or other personal identifiers
- We do not have access to your recordings, takes, notes, or any of your practice content
- We do not use location services
- We do not sell any data to third parties

## Recordings, Camera, and Microphone

Crescendo uses your microphone to record audio takes and your camera to record video takes. These recordings are captured and stored entirely on your device. We never upload, transmit, or access your recordings. You can grant or revoke camera and microphone access at any time in your device settings.

## Cloud Backup (Crescendo Pro)

If you subscribe to Crescendo Pro and enable backup, Crescendo creates backups of your data and media in **your own personal iCloud account** (on iOS). This storage belongs to you — your backups are kept within your Apple account, and we have no access to them and cannot read their contents. Restoring an existing backup is always available, even without an active subscription, so you are never locked out of your own data.

## Reference Videos (YouTube)

Crescendo lets you attach a YouTube reference video to a song. These videos are played through YouTube's embedded player. When you watch a reference video, your interaction is subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [YouTube's Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). We do not receive or store any data from the embedded player.

## Notifications

Crescendo can send local notifications to remind you to practice on the days and times you choose. These notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely on-device. No notification data is sent to external servers.

## In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play Store. We never see or store your payment details. Purchase validation is handled by RevenueCat as described above.

## Third-Party Services

| Service                       | Purpose                           | Data Sent                                               |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| PostHog                       | Analytics and error tracking      | Anonymous usage events, device info                     |
| RevenueCat                    | Purchase validation               | Anonymous transaction and device data                   |
| Apple iCloud                  | Optional backup (Crescendo Pro)   | Stored in your own iCloud account; not accessible to us |
| YouTube / Google              | Embedded reference video playback | Managed by Google when you play a video                 |
| Apple App Store / Google Play | Payment processing                | Managed by the platform                                 |

## Children's Privacy

Crescendo is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Data Security

Because your content stays on your device (and, if you enable backup, in your own iCloud account), it is protected by your device's and your Apple account's security. We recommend keeping your device and operating system up to date.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.
